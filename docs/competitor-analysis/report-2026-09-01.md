## 6. Premium/Business OMR pricing recommendation

**Grounding:** No first-party Oman agency-tier pricing exists for any competitor (Section 3). The recommendation below is built from three weaker signals plus market context, and is flagged **[Guessing]** as a starting point to validate, not a benchmarked number:

1. GCC per-listing boost pricing (OpenSooq Jordan ~8 OMR/day equivalent; Bayut/PF UAE ~51–205 OMR per boosted listing) shows agencies in this region already pay meaningfully for visibility — but per-ad, in richer markets than Oman.
2. Every competitor's pricing is sales-gated and opaque; a low, simple, published price is itself a differentiator worth pricing to be self-serve-viable rather than sales-call-viable.
3. Local agencies (e.g., Hilal Properties, ~900 units) are used to relationship-based/negotiated costs, not SaaS subscriptions — the price needs to clear "obviously worth it for a small team" without a sales call.

| Tier | Listings | Recommended price | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free | 3 | OMR 0 (unchanged) | Trial funnel, matches shipped default |
| **Premium** | 25 | **OMR 15–25/month** (or OMR 150–250/year, ~15% discount) | Roughly the cost of 2–3 boosted listings elsewhere per month; cheap enough to buy without a sales call — undercuts the opaque, sales-gated competitor norm |
| **Business** | Unlimited | **OMR 40–60/month** (or OMR 400–600/year) | Targets larger incumbents (Hilal-scale agencies); still well below what a UAE-scale portal charges for a handful of boosted listings, while meaningfully monetizing the biggest accounts |

**Recommendation:** publish these prices on `/list-with-us` once Thawani/PayTabs checkout exists, rather than gating them behind a sales contact — every competitor researched hides its price, so a visible OMR number is itself a positioning move, not just a monetization detail. Validate the actual numbers with 3–5 conversations with real Omani agencies before finalizing; this range is inferred from adjacent GCC signals, not measured from a direct Oman comparable.
