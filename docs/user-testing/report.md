# Oman Property Intelligence — user-testing report (2026-09-07)

**Verdict: Not ready** — there is no confirmed P0, but four P1 code bugs sit inside the core loop and were each reproduced by a second, independent tester: the expected rent that ignores flat size, the yield that ignores the real asking rent, the agency listings table that hides price and status on the phone, and the listing form that silently changes "Villa" to "Apartment" after a rejected submit. The rule we apply says three or more of those means Not ready.

A first-time user can already do a lot: browse neighbourhood prices, rents and yields, compare two areas side by side on a shareable link, run the three calculators, filter the listings, send an enquiry as a guest, register, save favourites, and — as an agency — read and answer buyer enquiries. What they cannot do is trust the investment figures on a listing (a small flat is given a big flat's rent, so it looks like the better buy), put a photo on a listing, or edit a listing once it has been sent for review. The single most important thing to change is the expected-rent calculation on the listing page: work it out from the flat's own size, not the neighbourhood average, and use the real asking rent when the listing has one [Certain — both figures were checked by hand and reproduced in English and Arabic].

**First impression: 74/100** — every tester understood the site in 5 to 15 seconds, all five said they would keep going, and the source badge on every number was named as the most reassuring thing on the page; the marks come off for three dead cards on the home page and for listings with no photos.

**Core loop: 66/100** — the buying journey runs end to end on both devices and the maths in the calculators is right, but the investment numbers on a listing mislead, and the agency side is missing photos and any way to correct a listing after sending it.

## Findings, most serious first

### P0

None confirmed. Three testers reported the most alarming thing we saw all day — pressing Save or Submit throws you back to the sign-in page and loses everything typed — but none of the three reproduced when re-tested independently. They are recorded below at P2 as "seen once, not reproduced".

### P1

**Expected market rent ignores the flat's size, so the smallest flat always looks like the best investment** (buyer, desktop and phone, English and Arabic, Certain)
What happened: on the Al Mouj listings, a 110 sqm two-bedroom at OMR 125,500 and a 75 sqm one-bedroom at OMR 85,500 are both given the same "expected market rent" of OMR 650 a month. Expected: the expected rent should follow the size or bedroom count of the flat. Seen: because the rent is the same, the small flat shows a 9.1% gross yield and an investment score of 82/100 "Strong", while the bigger flat shows 6.2% and 68/100 "Moderate" — and the neighbourhood page for the same area says 6.5%. Evidence: `screenshots/buyer-en/062-desktop-core-enquiry-listing-1br-almouj-yield.png`, `screenshots/buyer-ar/114-desktop-listing-compare2-listings-compare-result.png`. Confirmed by an independent re-test: yes. Fix brief: work out the expected rent for a listing from the neighbourhood's rent per square metre (or a per-bedroom band) multiplied by that listing's own size, then recalculate the gross yield and the investment score from it; add one line under the figure saying what it is based on. Until that exists, label the figure plainly as a neighbourhood average and leave it out of the investment score, so a one-bedroom is never scored on a rent it cannot achieve. The same code powers the listing detail page and the listing comparison, in both languages.

**Gross yield on a rental listing ignores the real asking rent shown one line above it** (buyer, desktop and phone, English, Certain)
What happened: open the Al Mouj four-bedroom golf-course villa, advertised at OMR 2,140 a month, and read the financial block. Expected: with a real asking rent on the page, the yield should be worked out from it — 2,140 × 12 ÷ 477,008 = 5.4% — or the page should say in one line why it uses an estimate instead. Seen: "Listed rent (asking) OMR 2,140" sits directly above "Expected market rent OMR 1,808 (AI estimated)", and the yield of 4.5% comes from the 1,808 estimate. Two rents, no explanation. Evidence: `screenshots/buyer-en/x-desktop-rental-listing2-rental-listing-financials.png`. Confirmed by an independent re-test: yes. Fix brief: when a listing carries its own asking rent, use it for the yield and the investment score, keep the estimate as a second line, and say which of the two the yield used. This is the site's own promise about honest numbers being broken by its own arithmetic.

**The ROI calculator says "estimated total return OMR 0" for anything longer than 30 years** (buyer, desktop and phone, English, Likely)
What happened: open the ROI calculator and set the investment horizon to 30 years — a total return of OMR 82,936 and a cash-flow chart appear. Change it to 31 (also tried 35, 36, 38, 40, 50 and 100). Expected: either a real projection, or the box capped at 30 years with a visible note. Seen: "Estimated total return after 31 years OMR 0", with the year the user typed echoed back, and the chart silently gone. Evidence: `screenshots/buyer-en/071-desktop-repro-f3-03-roi-years-31-zero-total-return.png` (the re-test shot; the original screenshot was lost). Confirmed by an independent re-test: yes. Fix brief: either extend the projection past 30 years or set a maximum of 30 on the years box with a short note beside it; never echo a year the calculator cannot handle while printing zero.

**After too many wrong passwords the Sign in button dies with no message at all** (buyer, desktop, English and Arabic, Certain)
What happened: enter a real email with a wrong password ten times — each attempt says "Incorrect email or password". From the eleventh attempt on, the button greys out, stays disabled for ten seconds or more, and nothing appears anywhere on the page. Expected: a plain sentence such as "Too many attempts. Try again in 15 minutes", and a button that comes back to life. Seen: a dead form with no feedback whatsoever, in both languages. Evidence: `screenshots/buyer-en/x-desktop-edge-lockout-msg-rate-limited-message.png`. Confirmed by an independent re-test: yes. Fix brief: the sign-in action already knows it has hit the limit — pass that back to the form and show it in the same red slot the wrong-password message uses, then re-enable the button when the wait is over. The limit itself is sensible; the silence is what makes people think the site is broken.

Expected: everything chosen comes back still selected, with a message saying the price is the problem. Seen: only the typed boxes keep their values. **There is nowhere for an agency to add photos to a listing** (agency, desktop and phone, English and Arabic, Certain)

**An agency cannot open, edit, correct or withdraw a listing it created** (agency, desktop and phone, Arabic and English, Certain)
What happened: sign in as an agency, open My listings, and try to click a row. Expected: the row opens the listing, with edit, withdraw and mark-as-sold. Fix brief: make each row open a listing page owned by that agency, with edit and withdraw actions limited to their own listings; an edited listing can go back into the review queue.

### P2

**Submitting a new listing signs the agency out and the listing is never saved** (agency, desktop and phone, English and Arabic — seen once, not reproduced)
What happened: fill the villa form and press Submit for review. Seen by both agency testers: the page turned into the sign-in form, the session cookie was deleted, and the listing never existed — reported six times each. Expected: the listing saved as "Pending review", still signed in. Evidence: `screenshots/agency-en/038-desktop-core-create-after-submit.png`, `screenshots/agency-ar/048-desktop-core-create2-after-submit-2.png`, `screenshots/agency-en/016-phone-blind-phone-create-31-after-submit.png`. Confirmed by an independent re-test: no (seen once). Three fresh re-tests on desktop and phone, in both languages, all created the listing cleanly, kept the session and showed the "pending review" row; the Arabic blind tester also created a listing successfully earlier the same day. The best explanation is a stale test server left over from before a restart, not the product [Likely]. Fix brief: no code change is proven necessary. Add one regression test that submits the listing form and checks both that a listing row was created and that the sign-in cookie survives, so if this ever comes back it is caught immediately. If it does recur on a freshly built server, treat it as the most serious bug in the app.

**Saving the agency profile signs the agency out and throws the edit away** (agency, desktop, English and Arabic — seen once, not reproduced)
What happened: change the agency phone number and press Save. Seen: the page became the sign-in form and the old number was still there afterwards. Evidence: `screenshots/agency-en/053-desktop-profile-enquiries-after-profile-save.png`, `screenshots/agency-ar/070-desktop-inbox-profile-profile-saved.png`. Confirmed by an independent re-test: no (seen once). Re-tests saved the profile every time and kept the session. The re-tester found the likely cause of at least the Arabic report: the page has two buttons that submit, and the first one in the page's own order is the header's "Sign out" — an automated script clicking "the first submit button" signs itself out [Certain about the two buttons, Likely about the cause]. Fix brief: leave the save alone, but move the header's sign-out button so it does not sit ahead of the page's own form; that also makes the page safer for anyone using a keyboard. Separately, the phone-number box silently refuses landline numbers with no message, which reads as a dead Save button — worth a proper error line.

**Every admin form with a Save button signs the admin out and saves nothing** (admin, desktop and phone, English — seen once, not reproduced)
What happened: sign in as admin, add a market statistic, press Save. Seen: the sign-in form replaced the page and no row was created; the same for adding a property, adding a listing and editing an existing property. Evidence: `screenshots/admin-en/061-desktop-core-stat-create-stat-after-submit.png`. Confirmed by an independent re-test: no (seen once). Two clean re-tests saved the figure, showed the green "Saved." banner and the new row, and kept the session; the admin's own blind run had also saved a figure successfully earlier that day. The same "Sign out is the first submit button on the page" explanation applies here [Likely]. Fix brief: as above — move the sign-out control out of the way of the page's own forms, and add a test that saves a market figure as admin and checks the row exists afterwards.

**The compare-listings dropdown shows monthly rents in the same shape as sale prices** (buyer, desktop and phone, English and Arabic, Certain)
What happened: open the listing comparison and read the dropdown. Expected: rentals marked, e.g. "OMR 2,140 / month" or a "For rent" tag. Seen: a four-bedroom Al Mouj rental villa reads as "OMR 2,140" three rows below a "OMR 125,500" flat. Evidence: `screenshots/buyer-en/x-desktop-listing-compare-listing-compare-result.png`. Confirmed by an independent re-test: yes — and the re-tester lowered it from P1 to P2, because the moment you pick an option the result card correctly says "For rent". Fix brief: add the "per month" suffix and the for-sale/for-rent tag to the dropdown labels, reusing the wording already used on the browse cards.

**The calculators accept impossible numbers and answer with a confident figure anyway** (buyer, agency and admin, desktop and phone, English and Arabic, Certain)
What happened: put a zero, a negative number or a twelve-digit number into the yield or ROI calculators. Expected: an inline message, like the mortgage calculator already gives ("Enter a rate up to 30%"). Seen: a negative purchase price prints "annual rent OMR 0.000" even though a rent was typed; a mortgage term of zero prints "monthly payment OMR 0.000" while the financed amount stays on screen; a negative interest rate shows the warning but still prints a monthly payment worked out at 0%; twelve digits print a 1,200% yield; the ROI calculator prints "break-even −1 months". Confirmed by an independent re-test: not re-tested (four of the five testers found it separately). Fix brief: give all three calculators the same rules the mortgage one has — price above zero, years between 1 and 30, deposit below the price, rate between 0 and 30% — with a short message beside the offending box, and hide the results while anything is invalid instead of quietly computing from a substituted zero.

**The compare page goes blank with no message when a neighbourhood is missing or misspelt** (buyer, desktop and phone, English and Arabic, Certain)
What happened: open a comparison link with only one area chosen, or with a typo in an area name. Expected: "Pick a second neighbourhood" or "We have no data for that area". Seen: the form appears and everything under it is empty — no cards, no chart, no message. With a bad name the first dropdown even displays a neighbourhood you never chose (it is just showing the first option). Confirmed by an independent re-test: not re-tested. Fix brief: show an explicit message under the form when either side is missing or unknown, and never let an unmatched value silently display as if it were selected.

**A wrong link shows a bare English "404" page with no way back** (buyer, desktop and phone, English and Arabic, Certain)

**The enquiry form keeps an old error message that blames boxes you have already fixed** (buyer, desktop, English, Certain)

## Owner setup needed before launch (not code bugs)

- **Real market data.** Every price, rent and yield on the site today carries the same "AI estimated, 35% confidence" badge, while the home page promises verified, official and user-submitted figures too. Every tester noticed, and two said it was the reason they would not act on the numbers. Load at least one official or verified source for the headline neighbourhoods — or soften the home-page promise until one exists. Shows today on the market dashboard, every neighbourhood page, the comparison and every listing's financial block.
- **Photos on the sample listings.** All fifteen listings show "No photos yet", which makes the marketplace look unfinished to a first-time visitor. Add photos to the seeded listings, or replace the empty grey block with a neighbourhood picture. (The agency side also needs the upload built — that is the P1 above.)
- **The AI analyst key.** The analyst replies "not switched on yet" and says so honestly in both languages; setting `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` on the host turns it on.
- **Enquiry emails.** Sending the enquiry on to the agency by email is still a to-do in the code, so today a buyer sees "your enquiry has been sent" and it only appears inside the site. The owner needs to choose and pay for an email service before this can be finished.
- **A business email address on the Privacy and Terms pages.** Both pages, in both languages, currently publish a personal Hotmail address as the data-protection contact on a page that cites Oman's Personal Data Protection Law. Create a mailbox on the site's own domain and swap it in.
- **Agency payments.** There is no card payment for agency tiers; the owner grants tiers by hand from the admin console. Fine for a soft launch, but a decision is needed before charging agencies.
- **Password reset.** Not built. The sign-in page offers only "New here? Create account", so a locked-out user has no way back in. Worth scheduling before real accounts exist.
- **The anti-spam widget keys.** The Turnstile challenge on the enquiry and register forms has no keys set, so it is invisible and could not be tested.

## Things that looked wrong but are not bugs

- **The blank map** on listing and neighbourhood pages: map tiles are blocked in this test sandbox by design, so only the pin, the zoom buttons and the attribution draw. Not an app fault.
- **The "AI estimated, 35%" badge itself** is the honesty feature working as intended — the sample data is labelled correctly. What is missing is any higher-trust source behind it, which is the owner-setup item above.
- **The AI analyst saying it is switched off** is the intended message for a dormant switch, and nothing else on the page broke.
- **"Insufficient data" instead of a rent figure** on some listings — this is the site refusing to invent a number, which is exactly the house rule, and testers named it as a delight.
- **A pending listing not appearing on the public browse page** is correct: nothing an agency posts goes live until the owner approves it, and a tester verified it does not leak.
- **The three sign-out reports** in the P2 list: strong evidence at the time, but no re-test could reproduce them, and the most likely explanations are a stale test server and an automated script clicking the header's Sign out button by accident.

## What worked well

- The neighbourhood comparison answers the exact question a yield buyer has, in one screen, with a link that can be shared and survives a reload.
- Every number the testers checked by hand was arithmetically right: 650 × 12 ÷ 120,000 = 6.5%; a 96,000 mortgage at 5% over 25 years = OMR 561.206 a month with 72,362 of interest; net yield 5.8% after 800 of costs.
- Money is handled correctly everywhere — three decimals where fractions exist, whole rials on market prices, Western digits inside the Arabic pages, and an Oman-specific ownership-eligibility field nobody expected.
- The enquiry flow is honest and forgiving: guests can send without signing up, the phone check names the problem precisely, three rapid clicks create exactly one enquiry, and refreshing afterwards does not send it again.
- The agency enquiry inbox is the best screen in the app — buyer name, email, phone, message and date with three plain actions — and a real buyer enquiry arrived with every detail exactly as typed.
- The admin review queue is a single "everything waiting on you" page, and publishing works end to end: the item leaves the queue, shows as Active, appears publicly, and the count drops by one.
- Privacy between accounts is clean: a second buyer sees none of the first's favourites or enquiries, a brand-new agency sees none of Bay Estates' data, and the agency and admin areas turn away anyone without the right role.
- No crashes anywhere: zero console errors, zero page errors and no failed requests across roughly seventy test runs, with the market dashboard still loading in about four seconds on a throttled 3G connection.

## What we could not test, and why

- Whether an enquiry actually reaches the agency by email — sending is still a to-do in the code, so the buyer only sees the on-screen confirmation and the entry in their account.
- Listing photos, the gallery and pinch-to-zoom — every listing shows "No photos yet", so there is no image to open.
- The AI analyst's actual answers — the key is not set, and it says so.
- Password recovery after being locked out — the feature does not exist.
- The anti-spam challenge on the enquiry and register forms — its keys are unset, so the widget is invisible.
- Map panning, zooming and pin accuracy — tiles are blocked in this sandbox.
- Rejecting an agency listing, marking a property verified, and approving or un-approving an agency — the only available records belonged to other testers' runs and those actions cannot be undone, so the admin tester left them alone.
- Seeing an agency's own listing go live publicly, and editing or removing a listing — blocked at the time by the sign-out report and by the missing edit controls.
- Agency tier upgrades and payments — not built; the owner switches tiers by hand.
- The isolation checks and the calculator edge cases on the phone for the English agency — time ran out; both were covered on desktop and the rules are enforced on the server.

## Phone verdict

This is a desktop-first site and it says so, but the phone came out better than expected. Every page fits a 390-pixel screen with no sideways scrolling, and a buyer can do the whole journey on a phone — market figures, neighbourhood, comparison, calculators, listing, enquiry, favourites and account — while an admin can clear the whole review queue one-handed. Two things spoil it. First, the small controls: the header's Favourites, Account and Sign out links are 20 pixels tall and sit four pixels apart, so testers repeatedly signed themselves out by accident; the favourites heart is 33 by 28; the admin's Publish and Reject buttons are 32 pixels and sit side by side, and Reject cannot be undone. Second, the wide tables: the agency's My listings and the admin's market statistics are desktop tables inside a silent side-scroller, so price, status, source and the delete button are simply invisible — the one thing an agent opens their phone to check is the thing they cannot see [Certain]. Fix the tap targets and turn those two tables into stacked cards and the phone becomes a genuinely good second screen rather than a shrunken first one.

## Arabic verdict

The Arabic version is not a translation bolted on; it reads like it was built for Oman. One tap in the header flips the entire site — public pages, the agency workspace and the admin desk alike — into correct right-to-left layout, with mirrored back arrows, Arabic month names, natural wording, and money kept in Western digits with three decimals as the house rules require. Across thirteen Arabic screens the tester found no raw translation codes and no half-English page. Four small things let it down: the account page prints the raw system codes "USER"/"FREE" and "AGENCY"/"PREMIUM" in the middle of fluent Arabic, on the very first page you see after signing in; number and noun agreement is wrong on every listing card and in the ROI results ("1 غرف", "بعد 10 سنة"); a wrong link drops you onto a bare English "404" page with no header and no way back; and an English message inside an Arabic enquiry card puts its full stop on the wrong side. None of those is hard to fix, and none of the Arabic-only issues blocked a journey [Certain].

## Where testers disagreed

- **The silent lockout.** The two buyer testers both hit a wall after about ten wrong passwords — the button dies, or the message simply disappears, with nothing explaining why. The two agency testers and the admin tester each tried twelve wrong passwords and saw the normal "incorrect email or password" every time, with the correct password still working straight afterwards. Both accounts are recorded as reported; the limit may be counted per email address rather than per run of attempts, and the buyers simply pushed further (15 and 18 attempts) [Guessing].
- **The listing submit and the admin save.** Three testers reported being signed out with their work lost, with cookie evidence; three independent re-tests could not reproduce it at all, and both the Arabic agency's and the admin's own blind runs had saved successfully hours earlier. Both sides are shown above: reported as fatal, re-tested as working.
- **Empty trend charts.** The English buyer noted the compare chart takes about two seconds to draw its lines on a phone and photographed an empty frame; the Arabic buyer states flatly that the lines draw within about 0.8 seconds on both devices and that the "empty" screenshot actually shows them. Most likely a difference in when the picture was taken [Likely].
- **The admin statistics table on the phone.** The blind admin tester called it "unusable, prices sliced off"; the full admin run measured it and called that too harsh — the page itself does not overflow, the table scrolls inside its own box. Both agree the source column and the delete button are unreachable without discovering a hidden sideways scroll.
- **The phone chart pop-up.** The Arabic blind tester found the tap pop-up covers most of the chart; the same persona's full run could not make a pop-up appear on a phone at all in three attempts.
